# Md. Anisul Islam

### Registered Pharmacist | Computational Drug Discovery Researcher | Bioinformatics Enthusiast

## About Me

I am a Bachelor of Pharmacy graduate and researcher interested in computer-aided drug design, molecular docking, molecular dynamics simulation, bioinformatics, and natural product-based drug discovery.

My research focuses on integrating experimental pharmacology with computational approaches for identifying potential therapeutic candidates.

## Research Interests

🧬 Computer-Aided Drug Design (CADD)

🧪 Molecular Docking & Virtual Screening

⚛️ Density Functional Theory (DFT)

💻 Molecular Dynamics Simulation

🧬 Network Pharmacology

🧠 Neurodegenerative Disease Research

🎗️ Cancer Biology & Drug Discovery
